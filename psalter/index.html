Signed in? The pages open from the abbey's private Google Drive folder. If you see “You need access,” sign in with the Google account the psalter was shared with, or ask Br. Augustine to share it with you.

Tap the week the choir is on. This phone remembers it and moves on to the next week every Sunday. Until you choose, the page guesses from the calendar (Week I begins after Christmastide and again at Easter).
