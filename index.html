Sing With Us

Pray the Psalms with the Benedictine monks of this abbey.

The abbey church, Saint Bernard Abbey · © Saint Bernard Abbey

Welcome! Several times every day, the monks of this abbey come into this church to pray the Psalms. The Psalms are the 150 prayer-songs in the middle of the Bible. People have prayed them for thousands of years.

Almost every one of these prayer times is open to the public. That means you are welcome to join us. See the times.

Here is how it works. Two monks stand and sing a verse. Then the whole community answers with the next verse. Back and forth it goes, verse by verse, like a conversation. I'm one of the monks here, and I've found it can become exactly that: a conversation with God.
