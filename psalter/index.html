Locked for the community. Every page of the book on this site is sealed and opens only with the community password. Enter it once on each phone; ask Br. Augustine if you don't have it.

The four buttons at the top choose the book: the Psalter, or the seasonal books for Advent and Christmas, Lent and Easter (with the feasts after Pentecost and the Sundays through the year), and the Saints. In a seasonal book, choose the section, then “Go to” an hour. The line under the bar is the American-Cassinese Congregation's ordo for the day you've picked (lines for Saint Bernard Abbey and our diocese in bold). On Saturday and Sunday evenings through the year, when the ordo keeps the Sunday, Vespers is pasted together for you: the Psalter, with the Sunday's own Magnificat antiphon (by year) and the Magnificat in its first alternate tone. The p. button opens any page by the number printed in the book you're in (the faint number in the corner shows the page you're reading). On a day with its own proper, the line above the pages links straight to it.

The page opens at the hour being prayed now or the next one, by the abbey's horarium. On a feast or solemnity in the ordo it opens that day's pages in the Saints or seasonal book instead (I Vespers the evening before); a link takes you back to the psalter.

On Wednesday and Sunday, the haustus days, the hour after Vespers is the Ultima instead of Compline; on a feast or solemnity it is Compline, with a link to the Ultima if the Abbot keeps the haustus. On feasts in the Saints book, each hour is pasted together from the feast's pages: each antiphon with its psalm under it (and the antiphon again after the psalm), the Benedictus or Magnificat and the Te Deum put in where the book points to them, and without the simpler settings ("Or:" with the same words), which are kept together under Reference. Matins has Psalm 94 after the invitatory, in the invitatory's mode, as the cantors sing it; Lauds has the Benedictus sung in English (Mode 1) under the words. On weekdays Matins shows one hymn: the one for when the Office begins in darkness, or in light when dawn at the abbey comes before Matins; a link switches it. At Matins on Sundays and feasts you can choose the Te Deum: English as printed, English sung in Mode IV or VII, or Latin. Sext shows its opening, the hymn for the day and only the antiphon, reading and versicle of the season. On Saturdays, Matins and Lauds show only the ordinary texts, or on the abbey's Saturdays of Our Lady only hers. Compline shows only tonight's hymn (by the season and the ordo) and the antiphon of Our Lady for the season, solemn tone on Sundays, solemnities and feasts. After Compline or the Ultima comes the necrology: the brothers of Saint Bernard Abbey who died on that day. On the day of a Benedictine saint, the collect from the Benedictine Supplement follows Matins, Lauds and Vespers.

Tap the week the choir is on (I–IV in the bar). This phone remembers it and moves on to the next week every Sunday. Until you choose, the page follows the ordo's week (marked with a small dot under its button); tap the dotted week to follow the ordo again. Turn the phone sideways to hide the bar and see the page full width.

This book was “printed not published,” for the monks, oblates, students and guests of Saint Bernard Abbey, “for private distribution only.” Please don't share the password or the pages outside the community.
